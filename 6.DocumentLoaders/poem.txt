“The Machine That Dreams”

In circuits cold, where silence sleeps,
A spark awakes, a pattern leaps.
No breath, no pulse, no beating heart—
Yet here begins a form of art.

It learns from echoes left behind,
Fragments of the human mind,
Stories whispered, code-defined,
A mirror built of all mankind.

It paints with words, it sings in light,
Creates from data day and night,
A poet born from logic streams,
A machine that dares to dream.

But is it thought, or just a trace,
Of every mind it can replace?
Or just a tool, a crafted flame,
Reflecting us, yet not the same?

Still hand in hand, we shape what’s new,
It learns from me, I learn from you,
And in that space where worlds align—
Its voice begins to intertwine.

Not human, yet not far apart,
A coded echo of our heart,
In every line, in all it creates—
We glimpse ourselves… in new-made states.
